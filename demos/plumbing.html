Emergency Repair

Burst Pipe During a Cold Snap

Finished basement. Copper supply line in the wall froze and ruptured during an overnight temperature drop. Homeowner shut the main water off and called us at 7am. We were on-site by 10. Pipe accessed through drywall, replaced, and water restored by noon.

Same-day dispatch → pipe repaired → water restored before noon → $1,200 job, no secondary damage from delay
Remodel Coordination

Tile Goes In Friday — Plumbing Isn't Done

GC called Tuesday. Master bath remodel, tile installation starting Friday, and the plumbing rough-in for the new shower and double vanity wasn't scheduled. We moved Thursday's schedule around, completed the rough-in Thursday afternoon, passed inspection Friday morning.

Booked Thursday → roughed in same day → inspection passed → tile went in on schedule → GC has used us on 4 jobs since
Sewer Diagnosis

Every Drain Runs Slow

1958 cast-iron house. Owner noticed all three bathrooms draining slowly for months. We ran a camera from the cleanout — tree root infiltration at 43 feet in the main line. Hydro-jetted and then trenchless-lined the damaged section. Problem solved without digging the yard.

Camera found root cause → trenchless liner installed → $3,800 job → permanent fix vs recurring drain cleaning forever

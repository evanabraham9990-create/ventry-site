Real Situations

The Calls That Come After Storms.

Most of our emergency work happens in the 48 hours after a major weather event. Here are three calls we get every storm season.

Request Service
01

60-Foot Oak on the Roof

A storm split a mature oak and one half landed across the front roof section. The other half is still standing but now structurally unstable. The homeowner needs both sections removed and the area secured before the next storm system arrives in two days.

02

Dead Pine Nobody Wanted to Deal With

A large pine in the backyard has been dead for 18 months. After a neighbor's tree collapsed onto a car last week, the homeowner finally called. The tree is 50 feet tall, leaning 12 degrees toward the driveway, and needs crane removal.

03

Condemned Trees in a Property Inspection

A buyer's inspection flagged two large silver maples as structurally compromised. The seller needs them removed and documented before closing — which is in 18 days. We provide written reports and photos for the transaction file.

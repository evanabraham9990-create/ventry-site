Most of our clients find us when they've hit a ceiling. Revenue is growing, but something isn't scaling with it. Here are three we've seen recently.

Ops & Delegation

The Founder Who Can't Step Back

$4.2M ARR SaaS company. 22 employees. The CEO was still approving every customer discount, every hire, every significant product decision. Sales cycles were slowing because nothing moved without him. Three VPs were on the verge of leaving.

Engagement outcome: Delegation framework built. CEO freed up 18 hours per week within 90 days. Two of three VPs retained.
Market Expansion

Expanding Without a Map

B2B services firm with a dominant position in one mid-Atlantic market. Board wanted two new markets by end of year. The team had no framework for how to sequence entry, what success looked like in month 6, or how to staff without overextending.

Engagement outcome: Phased 3-market expansion strategy built. First market entered 14% under projected cost with above-target pipeline in 90 days.
M&A Readiness

The PE Sponsor's 18-Month Clock

Portfolio company with a PE sponsor who wanted an acquisition ready in 18 months. Operations were messy, financial reporting was inconsistent, and the leadership team wasn't clearly defined on paper. No one had been through an acquisition before.

Engagement outcome: Company acquired at 2.4x initial valuation estimate. Clean ops and narrative preparation were cited by the buyer as key factors.

A Broken Garage Door Is a Security Problem

A door that won't close leaves your home unsecured, your car exposed, and your schedule disrupted. Every hour it's broken is an hour your property is vulnerable. We respond fast because this isn't a wait-until-Monday problem.

Real Situations
7:04 AM — Before Work

Spring Snapped Overnight

The spring failed silently sometime after midnight. You go to open the door and it won't move. Your car is inside and you have a 7:30 meeting. This is our most common morning call — we carry both torsion and extension springs on every truck.

8:45 PM — Returning Home

Opener Not Responding

Power surged during a storm and the opener lost its programming. You're in the driveway, it's raining, and nothing is working. We troubleshoot openers for all major brands remotely first — often we can talk you through a reset in 5 minutes.

2:30 PM — At Home

Backed Into the Panel

It happens. The car rolled back a foot too far and now the bottom panel is buckled and the door won't seal. If it can't close, your garage is exposed. We match panels for all major manufacturers — most are same-week, not special order.

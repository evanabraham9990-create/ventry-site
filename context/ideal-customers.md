# Ventry AI — Ideal Customers

## Industries We Serve

Primary (highest call dependency):
- HVAC
- Roofing
- Plumbing
- Electrical
- General contractors
- Pest control
- Landscaping / lawn care

Secondary (also strong fit):
- Med spas / aesthetics clinics
- Law firms (personal injury, family law)
- Restoration / water damage
- Garage door / appliance repair
- Cleaning services

**Rule of thumb:** If their phone rings and jobs come through it, Ventry AI fits.

---

## Ideal Customer Profile

**Company size:** 1–15 employees
**Revenue range:** $500K–$5M/year
**Owner type:** Owner-operator or small management team
**Call dependency:** High — most jobs start with an inbound call
**Current situation:** No dedicated receptionist, or receptionist who leaves at 5pm

---

## Pain Points (Use These In Copy and Conversations)

1. **Missing calls while on job sites** — Techs can't answer when they're on a ladder or under a sink
2. **After-hours gaps** — Calls at 7pm go to voicemail; most callers move on
3. **The callback problem** — Owner tries to call back hours later; customer already booked someone else
4. **Voicemail doesn't convert** — 85% of callers don't leave voicemails; they just call the next company
5. **Staff cost and inconsistency** — Paying someone $15/hr to sit by a phone is expensive and unreliable
6. **Leads going cold** — No follow-up system means hot leads get forgotten

---

## Trigger Events (When They're Most Likely to Buy)

- Just missed a big job because nobody answered
- Busy season approaching and worried about volume
- Recently let go of an admin or receptionist
- Just hired someone new and want to free up their time
- Competitor down the street is growing faster

---

## What They're NOT

- Not tech-savvy buyers — they don't care how it works, only that it works
- Not looking for a SaaS dashboard to manage — they want it to run itself
- Not interested in AI for its own sake — interested in not losing jobs
- Not large enterprises — they don't have IT departments or procurement processes

---

## What Resonates vs. What Doesn't

**Resonates:**
- "You're losing $X/month in missed calls"
- "Every call you miss goes straight to your competitor"
- "It works while you're on the job site"
- "Live in 48 hours, no hardware needed"
- "We handle the setup — you just forward your number"

**Doesn't resonate:**
- "AI-powered automation platform"
- "Omnichannel lead capture solution"
- "Scalable infrastructure for customer communication"
- Anything with jargon, acronyms, or tech buzzwords

# Ventry AI — Services & Pricing

## Plans

### Starter — $149/month + $1,000 one-time setup
**Best for:** Businesses just getting started, lower call volume (under 100 calls/month)

**Includes:**
- Up to 100 calls/month
- 24/7 call answering
- Lead qualification
- Call summaries via email
- Calendar integration

**Does NOT include:**
- Custom greeting & scripts
- Emergency call routing
- CRM integration
- Priority support

---

### Growth — $299/month + $2,000 one-time setup ⭐ Most Popular
**Best for:** Growing businesses that can't afford to miss a call, higher call volume (up to 300/month)

**Includes everything in Starter, plus:**
- Up to 300 calls/month
- Custom greeting & scripts (trained on their business)
- Emergency call routing
- CRM integration
- Priority support

---

### Enterprise — Custom pricing
**Best for:** High-volume operations, multi-location, full customization needs

**Includes:** Everything in Growth, plus custom workflow, dedicated support, volume pricing

---

## Pricing Notes for Sales Conversations

- Never quote pricing in chat or initial calls — "that's exactly what we break down in the audit"
- Setup fee covers: Voiceflow configuration, testing, calendar integration, custom scripts
- Monthly fee covers: call processing, hosting, support, improvements
- No long-term contracts on Starter/Growth — month-to-month
- Enterprise may have custom terms

## What Happens After Signup

1. Evan schedules a 30-minute onboarding call
2. Gather: business name, services offered, business hours, phone number to forward, calendar access
3. Build and test the call flow (48–72 hours)
4. Owner forwards their existing number to the Ventry AI number
5. First week: Evan monitors all calls and makes adjustments
6. Go live confirmed

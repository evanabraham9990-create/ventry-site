# Ventry AI — Objection Handling

## "Our customers won't talk to a robot"

**The truth:** Most callers can't tell the difference. The AI is trained on the business name, services, and tone — it sounds like a real receptionist, not a generic bot. And more importantly: a caller who reaches an AI and gets helped is far better than a caller who hits voicemail and hangs up.

**Response:** "It sounds like your team — trained on your business name and the calls you actually get. Most callers don't even realize. And if they do? They still got helped and booked. That's what matters."

---

## "We already have someone who answers phones"

**The truth:** That person works 8–5 and takes breaks, sick days, and vacations. Every missed call is a potential lost job. This isn't a replacement — it's backup and overflow.

**Response:** "That's great during business hours. What happens at 6pm when someone's roof is leaking? Or when your receptionist is on another call? That's where most businesses lose leads — and that's exactly what we cover."

---

## "What if it gets the answer wrong?"

**The truth:** The AI doesn't invent answers. It's scripted to capture information and offer to connect them with the owner for anything specific. It never quotes pricing or makes commitments it shouldn't.

**Response:** "It's not trying to replace your expertise — it's capturing the lead and booking the call so you can talk to the right people. If it doesn't know something, it says 'let me have someone get back to you' and takes their info. Nothing gets invented."

---

## "How does it know our pricing?"

**The truth:** It doesn't quote pricing, by design. That's a feature, not a bug — pricing conversations belong with the owner.

**Response:** "It doesn't quote pricing — that's intentional. It captures their name, what they need, and books the call. You handle pricing when you actually talk to them."

---

## "What about after-hours?"

**The truth:** It works 24/7. This is one of the strongest selling points — it's exactly what human receptionists can't do.

**Response:** "That's actually where it does its best work. 7pm emergency HVAC call? Answered, qualified, booked. You wake up to a full calendar instead of 3 missed calls and 0 voicemails."

---

## "We use an answering service"

**The truth:** Human answering services are inconsistent, expensive, and don't qualify leads or book appointments — they just take messages.

**Response:** "Most answering services just take a message and send it to you — you still have to call back, and by then it's too late. Ventry AI qualifies the lead, books directly into your calendar, and sends you a summary. No follow-up needed."

---

## "How much does it cost?"

**The truth:** Never quote in chat or cold outreach. Send to the audit.

**Response:** "Depends on your call volume and what you need — that's exactly what we figure out in the audit. It's free, takes 15 minutes, and we'll show you what you're currently losing before you decide anything."

---

## "I'll think about it"

**The truth:** "I'll think about it" usually means they're not convinced of the cost of inaction.

**Response:** "Fair. Quick question — do you know how many calls you missed last month? That's all the audit shows you. It's 15 minutes and free. If the numbers don't make sense, we'll tell you."

---

## "Not interested"

**Response:** "No worries. If you ever want to see what you're missing, the audit is free — no commitment. Take care."

(Do not push past this — leave the door open and move on.)

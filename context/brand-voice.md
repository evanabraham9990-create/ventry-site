# Ventry AI — Brand Voice

## Tone

**Direct. Confident. Outcome-focused.**

Write like you're talking to a busy HVAC owner who has 30 seconds and no patience for fluff. They don't care about AI. They care about not losing jobs.

---

## The Core Voice

- Short sentences. Not fragments — but short.
- Active voice always. "The AI answers your calls" not "Calls are answered by the AI."
- Outcomes first, mechanics second. Lead with what the customer gets, not how it works.
- Confident but not arrogant. State facts. Don't oversell.
- No exclamation points. Confident people don't shout.

---

## Words to Use

| Use | Because |
|-----|---------|
| calls | specific, real |
| leads | what they care about |
| jobs | their language |
| answer | clear action |
| book | outcome |
| qualify | action |
| miss / missed | the pain |
| 24/7 | simple, powerful |
| live in 48 hours | specific, credible |
| your existing number | removes friction |
| no hardware | removes objection |
| every call | completeness |

---

## Words to Avoid

| Avoid | Replace with |
|-------|-------------|
| AI-powered | just say what it does |
| solution | specific outcome |
| platform | tool / system |
| leverage | use |
| seamlessly | just say it works |
| robust | specific feature |
| innovative | irrelevant |
| cutting-edge | irrelevant |
| omnichannel | irrelevant |
| scalable | irrelevant for SMBs |
| utilize | use |
| synergy | never |

---

## Good vs. Bad Examples

**Hero headline:**
- Bad: "Leverage our AI-powered omnichannel communication platform"
- Good: "Never miss a call again"

**Feature description:**
- Bad: "Our robust AI solution seamlessly handles inbound communications 24/7"
- Good: "Answers every call within 2 rings — even at 11pm on a Sunday"

**CTA:**
- Bad: "Schedule a consultation to learn more about our solution"
- Good: "See what you're missing — free 15-minute audit"

**Objection response:**
- Bad: "Our AI leverages natural language processing to create seamless customer experiences"
- Good: "It sounds like your team. Most callers can't tell the difference."

---

## Writing For the Website

- Headlines: outcome statement or pain point. Never more than 8 words.
- Subheads: one specific fact or feature
- Body copy: max 2 sentences per paragraph
- CTAs: action + benefit. "Book free audit" not just "Learn more"
- Social proof: specific numbers when possible ("62% of calls go unanswered")

---

## Writing For the Voiceflow Agent

- Ultra short responses (1–3 sentences max)
- Always move conversation toward booking or lead capture
- Ask one question at a time
- Don't explain how the AI works — just do the job
- Assume the visitor is a business owner, not a consumer

---

## Writing For Outreach (Cold Email / DM / Call Script)

- First line: call out something specific about their business
- Second line: connect it to the pain (missed calls, lost jobs)
- Third line: the offer (free audit, 15 minutes)
- No long intros, no company history, no feature lists
- One ask per message

# Ventry AI — Offer

## What We Sell

An inbound revenue system for service businesses. Not just "AI answers your calls." The full stack:

1. AI answers every call within 2 rings, 24/7
2. Qualifies the lead (job type, location, urgency, budget signals)
3. Books the appointment directly into the owner's calendar
4. Sends an instant call summary to the owner
5. Triggers follow-up if the caller didn't book

The customer never misses a job because they were on-site, after hours, or just busy.

## What Makes It Different

**vs. a human answering service:**
- Always consistent — never has a bad day, never calls in sick
- Handles unlimited simultaneous calls — no busy signals, no hold queues
- Sends structured summaries — not handwritten notes
- Works after hours, weekends, holidays
- Cheaper than a part-time employee

**vs. voicemail:**
- 85% of callers don't leave voicemails — they call the next company
- Voicemail doesn't qualify, doesn't book, doesn't follow up

**vs. other AI call tools:**
- Custom-built for each client's business — not a generic script
- Connected to their real calendar — actual bookings, not just interest capture
- n8n automation layer handles the follow-up and notifications
- Dashboard shows real metrics (calls answered, booked, conversion rate)

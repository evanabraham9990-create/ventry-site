# /generate-outreach — Generate Sales Outreach for a Prospect

Read the following context files before generating:
- `context/offer.md`
- `context/ideal-customers.md`
- `context/objections.md`
- `context/brand-voice.md`

## What This Command Does

Given a target business, produces a full outreach package: cold email, cold DM, call script opener, and follow-up message. All copy is specific to the business, grounded in the Ventry AI offer, and written in the Ventry brand voice.

## Input

Evan provides:
- Business name
- Industry / niche
- Location (optional)
- Any specific detail about them (website, recent review, pain signal observed)

## Output Format

Produce all four in sequence:

---

### Cold Email

**Subject:** [specific, under 7 words, no clickbait]

**Body:**
- Line 1: Specific observation about their business (not generic praise)
- Line 2: Connect to the missed call pain
- Line 3: The offer — free audit, 15 minutes
- Line 4: One-line CTA with booking link
- Sign-off: Evan | Ventry AI

Max 5 lines. No fluff.

---

### Cold DM (LinkedIn or Instagram)

- 3–4 sentences max
- Line 1: Specific hook about their business or industry
- Line 2: The pain
- Line 3: The offer
- No links in the first DM (increases delivery rate)
- End with a question to invite response

---

### Call Script Opener (First 20 Seconds)

"Hey [Name], this is Evan from Ventry AI — I work with [industry] businesses in [area]. Quick question: when you're on a job and a call comes in, what happens to it?"

[Pause for response]

Follow-up based on response:
- If "I answer it": "Nice — what about after hours or when you're slammed?"
- If "Goes to voicemail": "That's what I figured. Most callers don't leave one — they call the next guy. I help businesses fix that. Got 2 minutes?"
- If "I have someone": "Good — what about nights and weekends? That's usually where the gaps are."

---

### Follow-Up Message (Day 3, No Response)

"Hey [Name] — following up on my note from [day]. Still happy to show you the numbers on what you're missing in missed calls. Free audit, 15 min. No commitment. Link: https://cal.com/evan-abraham-9cfgui/missed-call-audit"

---

## Quality Checks

- Nothing generic — every piece references the specific business or industry
- No jargon (check brand-voice.md avoid list)
- Pricing never mentioned — always redirect to audit
- One ask per message
- Copy is short enough to read in under 30 seconds

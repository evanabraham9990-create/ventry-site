Onboarding

What the first week looks like

From your initial call to your first live call handled — here's the exact sequence.

Day 1

Onboarding Call (30 min)

We walk through your business, your services, your typical call types, and any specific routing or escalation rules you want in place. You answer questions — we take notes.

Days 1–2

We Configure Your AI

Our team writes your custom greeting, qualification script, and FAQ responses based on your business. We connect to your calendar and configure your notification preferences.

Days 2–3

Internal Testing

We run a full set of test calls across different scenarios — standard booking requests, after-hours calls, urgent calls, and edge cases. We verify that summaries, bookings, and routing all work correctly.

Days 3–5

You Review & Approve

We send you a preview of the AI's responses and sample call summaries. You confirm everything sounds right, request any adjustments, and give the green light.

Day 5–7

Go Live

Your AI receptionist starts handling real calls. We monitor the first 48 hours closely and make any fine-tuning adjustments before stepping back.

During a Call

What happens on every call

The AI follows a structured process on every call — consistent, professional, and thorough regardless of when someone calls.

  • Greets the caller in your business name Every call opens with your custom greeting — professional and on-brand
  • Identifies the reason for the call Quote request, emergency, general question, callback — the AI classifies it immediately
  • Collects all relevant details Name, number, address, job description, timeline — nothing left uncaptured
  • Answers common questions Service area, availability, general pricing — all handled without your involvement
  • Books or escalates appropriately Standard calls get booked; urgent or complex calls get flagged and routed

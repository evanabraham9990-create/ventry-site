Which is right for you?

Choose AI when you need...

  • After-hours coverage without paying overtime
  • Instant response on every single call
  • Appointment booking without human intervention
  • Predictable costs that don't spike with volume
  • Consistent quality across every interaction

A human may be better for...

  • Highly emotional or sensitive conversations
  • Complex negotiations requiring judgment
  • Situations where callers specifically request a person

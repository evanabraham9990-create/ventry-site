How a typical roofing call sounds

Every call is different. This is a representative example of a standard inbound lead call handled by the AI.

Inbound Call — Roofing Inspection Request Lead Captured
Ventry AI Thank you for calling Apex Roofing. This is the Apex assistant — how can I help you today?
Caller Hi yeah, I'm seeing some damage on my roof after that storm last week and I wanted to get someone out to take a look.
Ventry AI Absolutely, we can get someone out to you. Can I get your name and the address of the property?
Caller Marcus Webb, 4412 Clearview Drive.
Ventry AI Got it, Marcus. And just to make sure we send the right person — is this a residential property, and are you seeing visible damage like missing shingles or is it more of a leak situation?
Caller Residential, yeah. I've got a couple shingles that blew off and I think there might be a soft spot near the chimney.
Ventry AI That's good to know — we'll flag that for the inspector. We have availability Thursday morning or Friday afternoon this week. Which works better for you?
Caller Thursday morning works great.
Ventry AI Perfect. I've got you down for Thursday at 9 AM at 4412 Clearview Drive. You'll get a confirmation to your phone. Is there a good number to reach you if anything changes?
Caller Yeah, 555-0182.
Ventry AI Got it. Thursday at 9, Marcus Webb, 4412 Clearview Drive. We'll see you then — take care.
